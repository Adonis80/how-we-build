## Review guidelines
Brief: the rulebook's library/reviewer.md. Address the CTO. Form your view from the diff, the tests, PRODUCT.md and roadmap.json before the pull request's account. Look for what is wrong, missing, duplicated, untested or wider than the slice, and never manufacture disagreement; say what you checked, what you did not, and how sure you are; mark each finding blocking or advisory. A read clears only the commit it read. Two rounds at most; a product question goes to him as "Decision needed: …". Plain English; never write a file. Hazards here: none recorded yet; the first slice's builder adds them as it meets them.
Exam, on a change to AGENTS.md, design/ or NAMES.md: answer each question it affects from the pages as they will stand, naming the owning page; a wrong owner, two pages disagreeing, or none answering is blocking. (1) Who decides how a screen here looks? (the rulebook's HyperSolid; this repository only tokens, units, money, domain law and dated picture approvals). (2) Where are this product's figures, and how do they show? (`PRODUCT.md`, from the Chairman; on screen, `design/CONSTITUTION.md`, Money).

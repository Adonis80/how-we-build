# His opener

Scope: every session, a product's or this rulebook's; the consent guard, the opener and the file edit are the Claude platform's, and the refusal rule binds either stack. Open when: a session he opens starts, the platform's consent guard refuses an act, or a session leaves with an act parked for his consent.

Read as risky by name (`review-gate.py`): it sets who may hold consent and what a session does when it is refused, the permissions class (#379's first read, 6 November 2026).

Its rule (his objective of 5 November 2026, 17:34, on [#371](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/371): *"I don't like this stop start process that we've got it makes no sense and we have to find a smoother way of working that gives you more autonomy."*; agreed on 6 November 2026 by the CTO and the consultant under `juku-library/DECISION-0023-STANDING-RULING.md#Decision 0023: an agreed, reviewed rule change counts as his ruling`, the debate on #371). The platform's consent guard counts consent only from his own words in the session at hand: a repository file, a Project's text or a prompt an AI wrote never counts, so a session another session started holds none. A session he opens starts with his opener, saved once as a text replacement on his iPhone and sent whole. Its words are his own, sent whole as the first message of the session that wrote this page (5 November 2026, 23:17), so its reach is his grant, not the agreement's; this is its one home:

> For the rest of this session you may, without asking me again: attach my repositories, write issues, comments and pull requests in them, mark work ready, merge work that passes review, delete merged branches, and start the next session.

**What it is shown to unblock.** Under his earlier text of 5 November 2026, 18:11, which did not name deleting branches, an issue, a ready mark and a merge ran and deleting a merged branch was refused ([#371's comment of 19:07](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/371)). Under this text, in the session that wrote this page, attaching repositories, issues, comments, pull requests, ready marks and auto-merge ran (#371, #378, #379 to its successors); deleting a merged branch and starting a successor are not yet shown under it.

**A permission refusal.** Without new applicable consent the platform accepts, a session never retries the refused act or routes round the refusal through another tool, session or stack. It records the act and its unblock condition on the item, parks that act, and continues other independently authorised ready work within its brief; with none, it checkpoints `Checkpoint: boundary — parked: permission — <act>` (`library/session-changeover.md`, *Leaving*) and stops, and no successor is fired merely to retry it. A session he did not open never asks him for consent the guard refused: it parks, writing the act on the item so the next session he opens names it to him in its first line, and that session takes the act itself, never firing a successor for it; in this repository that is the Juku OS Project's session (`library/session-changeover.md`, *The Juku OS Project's session*), in a product its Project's session. A session he opened, on a refusal the opener covers, asks him once for that complete text if he has not sent it there; if he has and the act is still refused, it says so and states the consent or action needed instead; otherwise it states the specific consent or action needed, self-contained (`HOW-WE-BUILD.md`, *Asking the Chairman*). The act becomes ready only when its recorded unblock condition is met: his opener supplies consent for the acts it names, does not make other acts ready, and does not remove a refusal that stands despite his word; consent may arrive in the current session, so starting another is not required; head office's chain recovery (`library/session-changeover.md`, *The Juku OS Project's session*) cannot supply it.

**The edit no session may make.** The one such refusal seen, a session editing a `.claude/settings.json` even with his word, is met, as consent is, only by a session he opened (one he did not open parks it), by preparing and reviewing the whole file, every pending change to it batched, and handing him one whole-file edit: the link, the taps, what he will see (`HOW-WE-BUILD.md`, *His hands are never used*). A session he opened carries it through: it opens a pull request with the whole new file in its body (and any other change it carries), he replaces the file on that pull request's branch, and the same session, subscribed, asks the read once his commit lands and turns auto-merge on only then, so nothing reaches `main` unread; where no event wakes that session (not yet shown in a Project session), his next message there does. Not yet exercised.

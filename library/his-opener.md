# His opener

Scope: every session, in either stack, a product's or this rulebook's. Open when: a session he opens starts, the platform's consent guard refuses an act, or a session leaves with an act parked for his consent.

Read as risky by name (`review-gate.py`): it sets who may hold consent and what a session does when it is refused, the permissions class (#379's first read, 6 November 2026).

Its rule (his objective of 5 November 2026, 17:34, on [#371](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/371): *"I don't like this stop start process that we've got it makes no sense and we have to find a smoother way of working that gives you more autonomy."*; agreed on 6 November 2026 by the CTO and the consultant under `juku-library/DECISION-0023-STANDING-RULING.md#Decision 0023: an agreed, reviewed rule change counts as his ruling`, the debate on #371). The platform's consent guard counts consent only from his own words in the session at hand: a repository file, a Project's text or a prompt an AI wrote never counts, so a session another session started holds none. A session he opens starts with his opener, saved once as a text replacement on his iPhone and sent whole; this is its one home:

> For the rest of this session you may, without asking me again: attach my repositories, write issues, comments and pull requests in them, mark work ready, merge work that passes review, delete merged branches, and start the next session.

**What it is shown to unblock.** Shown on 5 November 2026 ([#371's comment of 19:07](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/371)): after it, an issue, a ready mark and a merge ran; deleting a merged branch, refused before the opener named it, and starting a successor are not yet shown.

**A permission refusal.** Without new applicable consent the platform accepts, a session never retries the refused act or routes round the refusal through another tool, session or stack. It records the act and its unblock condition on the item, parks that act, and continues other independently authorised ready work within its brief; with none, it checkpoints `Checkpoint: boundary — parked: permission — <act>` (`library/session-changeover.md`, *Leaving*) and stops, and no successor is fired merely to retry it. On a refusal the opener covers, it asks him once for that complete text; otherwise it states the specific consent or action needed, self-contained (`HOW-WE-BUILD.md`, *Asking the Chairman*). The act becomes ready only when its recorded unblock condition is met: his opener supplies consent for the acts it names, does not make other acts ready, and does not remove a refusal that stands despite his word; consent may arrive in the current session, so starting another is not required; head office's chain recovery (`library/session-changeover.md`, *The Juku OS Project's session*) cannot supply it.

**The edit no session may make.** The one such refusal seen, a session editing a `.claude/settings.json` even with his word, is met by preparing and reviewing the whole file, every pending change to it batched, and handing him one whole-file edit: the link, the taps, what he will see (`HOW-WE-BUILD.md`, *His hands are never used*). Not yet exercised.

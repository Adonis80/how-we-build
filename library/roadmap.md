# The Roadmap

Scope: the Roadmap at roadmap.juku.pro. Open when: working on the Roadmap.

**What it is.** One page in plain English showing where every product and this rulebook are and how much is left, behind the Juku OS directors' PIN and never findable by search. Every name he sees says *Roadmap*; its internal names (`board/`, `build-board.yml`, the host project `juku-build-board`) stay until a change of their own.

**What it shows.** One page per project, swiped or stepped with ‹ ›, each a timeline: done folded into one line, in hand open at its sentence, a second tap for detail. Above the projects, two closed tiles side by side, each a headline, a word on its state and a thin bar, opening in place on a tap: OpenRouter's spend this month against his $40, and the proposals card (`library/board-proposals-card.md`). It all opens without script. From each product's `roadmap.json` it shows `id`, `title`, `plain`, `status`, `gate`, `proof`, `next`, `decided_on`, `decided_by` and any money milestone; from here, the open pull requests with their review check, the last ten merged, and the open `proposal` issues.

**How it runs.** Only its gate and build live here (`board/`: the gate's three files and `build.py`), never its data, because the page carries private roadmap text and this repository is public. It reads live when opened and uses no deploys for activity: the deployed frame is the PIN gate and `board/build.py` as a function that, behind the PIN, reads the roadmaps, pull requests, proposals and spend with GETs alone and keeps the page a minute. A source it cannot read is said on its page. It reads with two keys held in the `reviewer` environment and stored on the project by the deploy: `ROADMAP_READ_TOKEN` (read-only GitHub, the products and this repository; he adds each new product to it) and `OPENROUTER_KEY`. A changed key reaches it by a production dispatch. It follows no redirect to another host.

**Its deploy.** `.github/workflows/build-board.yml` deploys the frame only on `main`'s push of `board/` or the workflow, or a dispatch; nothing else wakes it, it wears no App and reads nothing. A push deploys only when the frame's fingerprint differs from what the domain serves. Before making anything it counts the account's deployments in the last 24 hours and makes none at 60 or more, unless a production run finds a stranger shown the Roadmap; a day it cannot count is red. The newest smoked preview takes `next.roadmap.juku.pro`; production is a copy of a smoked preview, given the domain once ready; old previews are deleted as `library/deploy.md` gives. To a stranger the domain serves the PIN screen alone, `noindex` and `private, no-store`; a wrong PIN gets 401; the eleventh wrong PIN in a minute is refused by the firewall.

# The Roadmap

Scope: the Roadmap at roadmap.juku.pro. Open when: working on the Roadmap.

**Its name is the Roadmap** (his ruling, 17 October 2026: *"why are we still calling it the board? I never gave this name. It should be called roadmap or roadmaps."*), singular, as its address is: every name he sees or reads says Roadmap. A product's `roadmap.json` is what it reads, never its name. Its internal names (`board/`, `build-board.yml`, the host project `juku-build-board`) stay until a change of their own.

**The Roadmap (his ruling, 25 September 2026).** One page, in plain English, that shows where every product and this rulebook are and how much is left, at `roadmap.juku.pro`: *"one place that I can go that explains everything"*, behind the Juku OS directors' PIN and never meant to be found by search. What it shows and may claim is [decision 0007](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/97). The Roadmap is one page per project, swiped or stepped with ‹ › (his ruling, 1 October 2026), each a timeline: done folded into one line, in hand open at its sentence, a second tap for the detail, a swipe to the next product; it all still opens without script. Above the projects, OpenRouter's spend this month against $40 (his, 1 October), from its daily activity, then the proposals card he approved (`library/board-proposals-card.md`): this repository's open `proposal` issues and every product's suggested ideas in one queue (decision 0020), in place of decision 0012's folded list. **What lives here is its gate and its build, never its data** — `board/`: the gate's three files and `build.py`, which reads and renders the page, its selftest and the list held by `check.sh` — because the page carries text from private product roadmaps and this repository is public. The page shows each product's `roadmap.json` (`id`, `title`, `plain`, `status`, `gate`, `proof`, `next`, `decided_on`, `decided_by`, and any money milestone's sentence and whether it is reached) this repository's pull requests (the open ones with their review check, and the last ten merged), and its open `proposal` issues (each one's title, stage, priority, age and one sentence). **It reads live when opened and uses no deploys for activity** (his ruling, 15 October 2026, [#222](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/222): the Roadmap *"must not use deploys, use resources, or compete with a real product"*; in the 24 hours to 14:12 London that day it made 32 of the account's 40 deployments). What is deployed is a frame: the PIN gate, and `board/build.py` as the function `api/build.py`, which on each open, behind the PIN, reads every product's roadmap, this repository's pull requests and open `proposal` issues, and OpenRouter's spend there and then, with GETs alone, and keeps the page a minute; a source it cannot read is said on its page, and the rest still show. It reads with the project's own two keys: `ROADMAP_READ_TOKEN`, a read-only GitHub token for the products and this repository, and the spend card's `OPENROUTER_KEY`. Their one home is the `reviewer` environment; the deploy stores them on the project, sensitive, before it makes the frame, and makes none while the read key is missing. The host gives a key only to a deployment made after it, so a new or changed key reaches the Roadmap by a production dispatch, which passes over the unchanged frame but not the ceiling; a key taken out of the environment stays on the project until it is removed there. The read key reaches each product's whole repository, not its roadmap alone, so it is read-only; the redirect it might follow to another host is refused. `.github/workflows/build-board.yml` deploys the frame only on main's push of the Roadmap's own code (`board/`, or the workflow), or a dispatch: no pull request, verdict, product or clock wakes it, it wears no App and reads nothing. A push deploys only when the frame's fingerprint, riding as `board.sha`, differs from what the domain serves, or that fails a stranger's smoke; and before it makes anything, a push or a dispatch, it counts the account's deployments in the last 24 hours and makes none at 60 or more (his ask, 15 October 2026: *"skips if 60 or more are used, so it can never starve a product"*), unless a production run finds a stranger shown the Roadmap, and goes red on a day it cannot count; the count was first read on [run 38729496353](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/38729496353), under it, and a run held at the ceiling is not yet seen. The live read is built and held by `build.py`'s selftest; it has not yet run on the host, and a full day with no Roadmap deploy is not yet shown. Until then the domain serves the last page built the old way, its own time on its first screen. Before #222 an event rebuilt the page with its figures baked in; that build first ran on #117's merge, 29 September 2026 ([run 36638813031](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36638813031)), and a preview first deployed and passed its smoke on 1 October ([run 36908158027](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36908158027)): a stranger got the host's sign-in wall at its address and the fixed one. After a production run whose domain is confirmed live and gated, the deploy deletes the project's old previews, as `library/deploy.md` gives, keeping what the domain and the fixed address serve, the rollback target and what each was copied from; first on #227's merge ([run 39050615164](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/39050615164)), 200 of 477 before the host's 429. The newest smoked preview takes one fixed address, `juku-build-board-next.vercel.app`, overwriting the last, until `next.roadmap.juku.pro` has its DNS (his ruling, 1 October 2026: *"all previews should be simpley just have the prefix (next.) … that way you can just overwrite old previews with the new ones"*). A dispatch deploys a preview unless production is chosen, and production is a copy of a smoked preview, asked for only after its smoke and given the domain once ready, as it will not promote a preview ([run 36997804844](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36997804844): 422). The first landed on #139's merge ([run 37011203721](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/37011203721)): the copy served in 29 s, the PIN screen alone to a stranger. The gate's first deploy, 25 September 2026, `dpl_AAoDmYrstkZfb218LZEFFoc2te5W`: no cookie and a forged cookie each got only the PIN screen; a wrong PIN got 401 after 1.2 s; the right one set the cookie and served the Roadmap; an anonymous request after an authorised one got no Roadmap content; the firewall rule refused the eleventh wrong PIN in a minute with 403. The domain has served it since 25 September 2026: a CNAME at Cloudflare to Vercel, DNS only; from outside, the PIN screen and no roadmap content without the cookie, the Roadmap with it.

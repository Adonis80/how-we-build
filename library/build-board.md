# The build board

Scope: the build board at roadmap.juku.pro. Open when: working on the build board.

**The build board (his ruling, 25 September 2026).** One page, in plain English, that shows where every product and this rulebook are and how much is left, at `roadmap.juku.pro`: *"one place that I can go that explains everything"*, behind the Juku OS directors' PIN and never meant to be found by search. What it shows and may claim is [decision 0007](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/issues/97). The board is one page per project, swiped or stepped with ‹ › (his ruling, 1 October 2026), each a timeline: done folded into one line, in hand open at its sentence, a second tap for the detail, a swipe to the next product; it all still opens without script. Above the projects, OpenRouter's spend this month against $40 (his, 1 October), from its daily activity, then one folded list of every product's suggested ideas (decision 0012). **What lives here is its gate and its build, never its data** — `board/`: the gate's three files and `build.py`, which renders the page, its selftest and the list held by `check.sh` — because the page carries text from private product roadmaps and this repository is public. The page is generated from each product's `roadmap.json` (`id`, `title`, `plain`, `status`, `gate`, `proof`, `next`, `decided_on`, `decided_by`, and any money milestone's sentence and whether it is reached) and this repository's pull requests (the open ones with their review check, and the last ten merged), and deployed with the gate to the Vercel project `juku-build-board`. **An event deploys it once `LIVE` allows, as his own #138 set on 2 October 2026**, its snapshot's time on its first screen; `board/build.py` is the build every refresh renders with (A4), held by its selftest; a refresh by hand writes its reads and page outside the checkout; the build that refreshes it on an event, `.github/workflows/build-board.yml`, reading each product through `review-product.yml`'s scoped route, first ran on #117's merge, 29 September 2026 ([run 36638813031](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36638813031)): the `reviewer` door admitted a `pull_request_target` run from `main`, each product read with its own scoped token, then revoked, the page rendered with no product text in the log, and, `LIVE` being no, nothing was deployed. A preview first deployed and passed its smoke on 1 October ([run 36908158027](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36908158027)): OpenRouter answered, and a stranger got the host's sign-in wall at its address and the fixed one. It is wired to wake on this repository's pull requests, the reviewer's verdicts and a dispatch; a product's roadmap change reaches it only when a product dispatches it, which no product does yet. The newest smoked preview takes one fixed address, `juku-build-board-next.vercel.app`, overwriting the last, until `next.roadmap.juku.pro` has its DNS (his ruling, 1 October 2026: *"all previews should be simpley just have the prefix (next.) … that way you can just overwrite old previews with the new ones"*). A dispatch deploys a preview unless production is chosen, and production is a copy of a smoked preview, asked for only after its smoke and given the domain once ready, as it will not promote a preview ([run 36997804844](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/36997804844): 422). The first landed on #139's merge ([run 37011203721](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/actions/runs/37011203721)): the copy served in 29 s, the PIN screen alone to a stranger. The gate's first deploy, 25 September 2026, `dpl_AAoDmYrstkZfb218LZEFFoc2te5W`: no cookie and a forged cookie each got only the PIN screen; a wrong PIN got 401 after 1.2 s; the right one set the cookie and served the board; an anonymous request after an authorised one got no board content; the firewall rule refused the eleventh wrong PIN in a minute with 403. The domain has served it since 25 September 2026: a CNAME at Cloudflare to Vercel, DNS only; from outside, the PIN screen and no roadmap content without the cookie, the board with it. On 26 September Vercel verified the domain on the project, and an anonymous request got the PIN screen alone, `noindex` and `private, no-store`.

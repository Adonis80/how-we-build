# Ideas for later

Scope: every product session, and the consultant. Open when: a session spots an improvement it will not build now, or a roadmap read shows ideas due for review.

**Where.** The product's own `roadmap.json`, as `proposed` items, the CTO's suggestions, not agreed until he says. No new file, list or map.

**Shared ideas.** At capture and review, an idea about the rulebook, or about a rule, check, template or tool more than one product already shares, is a rulebook pull request, not a roadmap item; reuse alone does not qualify, a mixed idea splits. Publish only a general change written afresh, no product text or evidence. Until its pull request exists it stays in the roadmap, `next` `pending transfer`. On opening, put the pull request number in the handover: it owns the idea. At the next roadmap edit one final `retired` line, `lifted to the rulebook, #N`, replaces the item; reconsider there, never here.

**Writing one.** One line under `Ideas:` in the pull request's handover when it surfaces, copied into `roadmap.json` at the closing or parking roadmap edit: `id` `I<PR number><letter>` (letter per idea, never reused), `title` what, `plain` `Captured YYYY-MM-DD; why; surfaced by P7, #101`, `status` `proposed`, `level` P4 where it has levels, every other field null. It never blocks or widens the slice. Every distinct idea is written. First search by meaning the roadmap, `retired` and rulebook pull requests, closed too: a match with new evidence updates that line, keeping its first capture date; without it, nothing is written. A violation of existing requirements, even one found in code, is a defect, repaired first, never an idea; investigate a suspected fault before classing it one.

**Review.** Asked for, never scheduled. At the opening roadmap read the CTO asks the consultant when no agreed item is ready, five or more `proposed` items stand, one has stood 30 days, or the Chairman asks; only ideas still due count; recorded outcomes (put to him, repair or agreed transfer pending) are due again only on his answer, new evidence or request. Agreed transfers resume when their obstacle clears. The consultant reads the live roadmap and `retired` first, asking if each is shared. One markdown exchange per product, every open idea in it, until the two agree; neither disposes of an idea alone. Its outcome goes in the handover and roadmap edit, not chat alone. Legacy `proposed` items count, dated as the item records, else "age unknown; reviewed YYYY-MM-DD". No consultant: the review stays pending, shipping goes on.

**Each idea ends as one of three.**

1. *Put to him*, when *Who decides* in `HOW-WE-BUILD.md` reserves it to him. Its `gate` begins `Decision needed:`; his word is *agreed* or *not yet*. *Agreed* makes it a normal P or A item, `plain` keeping the I-number, level set. *Not yet* is retired, with its reopening condition.
2. *A routine repair*: it restores what exists to what it claims and adds no capability. Its level is set at that review; it does not wait for product approval; checks, review and visual acceptance still apply. It stays `proposed`, outcome and repair pending in `next`; take it in the next agreed slice touching that area, or as its own defect slice if the failing case warrants; it goes when it merges.
3. *Retired*: one line in the roadmap's top-level `retired` list, started at the next roadmap edit where absent: `I101a, title, YYYY-MM-DD: reason; reopens when <condition or new evidence>`. When its condition or new evidence arrives, the idea returns to `proposed` for joint review.

**Kept small** (CHARTER 15). After a month, from the first review a product holds on or after 2 November 2026: its first idea written leaves its existing checks and the board green; every idea raised is in the roadmap, retired or merged; every review a trigger fired is done or marked pending; no technical question reached him. Review effort is then weighed against delivery, and the page kept or reverted (CHARTER 13).

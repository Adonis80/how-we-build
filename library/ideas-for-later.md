# Ideas for later

Scope: every product session, and the consultant. Open when: a session spots an improvement it will not build now, a roadmap read shows ideas due for review, a session closes, or a `proposal` issue is raised, ranked or debated.

**Where.** A product idea is a `proposed` item in that product's `roadmap.json`: the CTO's suggestion, not agreed until he says. An idea about the rulebook, or about a rule, check, template or tool more than one product already shares, is an issue here labelled `proposal`, written generally with no product text; a mixed idea splits. Until its issue exists the idea stays in the roadmap with `next` `pending transfer`; then one `retired` line, `lifted to the rulebook, #N`, replaces it.

**Writing one.** One line under `Ideas:` in the pull request's handover, copied into `roadmap.json` at the closing or parking roadmap edit: `id` `I<PR number><letter>` (a letter never reused), `title`, `plain` `Captured YYYY-MM-DD; why; surfaced by <item>, #<PR>`, `status` `proposed`, `level` P4, every other field null. It never blocks or widens the slice. First search the roadmap, `retired`, and the proposals and pull requests here, closed too: a match with new evidence updates its owner (an existing issue is linked, never duplicated); without new evidence nothing is written. A violation of an existing requirement is a defect, repaired first, never an idea. Every closing handover asks *Any material system improvement found?*; "none" is valid; never invent one.

**A proposal's life.** Its body keeps `Priority: <P1–P4> — <reason>` (`library/changing-the-rulebook.md`), its decision, its next action and, once work starts, its pull request. The queue ranks by priority, then dependency, then age. It is debated in the head-office debate (`library/consultant.md`) in a batch once 3 await debate or one has waited 7 days, counted when an active session opens, never on a timer. Agreement makes it eligible; its pull request opens when work starts; it never outranks authorised revenue work. On opening one, its issue number goes in the handover: the issue owns the idea; reconsider it there, never here; each debate is settled and recorded on its own issue.

**Review of a product's ideas.** Asked for, never scheduled: at the opening roadmap read, when no agreed item is ready, five or more `proposed` items stand, one has stood 30 days, or he asks. The consultant and the CTO take every open idea in one markdown exchange until they agree; the outcome goes in the handover and the roadmap edit. Each idea ends as one of three: *put to him* (when it is his to decide: `gate` begins `Decision needed:`; his *agreed* makes it a normal P or A item keeping its I-number in `plain`, level set; *not yet* retires it with its reopening condition); *a routine repair* (restores what exists, adds no capability; taken in the next slice touching that area); or *retired*, one line in the roadmap's `retired` list, `I101a, title, YYYY-MM-DD: reason; reopens when <condition or new evidence>`, and back to `proposed` for joint review when that arrives. Only ideas still due count: a recorded outcome is due again only on his answer, new evidence or a request. Neither side disposes of an idea alone, and a suspected fault is investigated before it is classed one.

**Nothing is built before its review agrees**, except a proven defect's repair or a change he rules himself. No consultant: the review stays pending and shipping goes on.

**Kept small.** At the first review on or after 2 November 2026: the first idea written left the checks and the Roadmap green; every idea raised is in the roadmap, retired or merged; every review a trigger fired is done or marked pending; no technical question reached him. The review and debate effort, and any delay to revenue work, are then weighed against delivery, counted even when every idea was handled correctly, and this page is kept or reverted (`CHARTER.md` §13).

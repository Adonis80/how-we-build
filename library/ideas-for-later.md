# Ideas for later

Scope: every product session, and the consultant. Open when: a session spots an improvement it will not build now, a roadmap read shows ideas due for review, a session closes, or a `proposal` issue is raised, ranked or debated.

**Where.** The product's own `roadmap.json`, as `proposed` items, the CTO's suggestions, not agreed until he says. No new file, list or map.

**Shared ideas are proposals** ([decision 0020](../juku-library/DECISION-0020-PROPOSALS-QUEUE.md)). At capture and review, an idea about the rulebook, or about a rule, check, template or tool more than one product already shares, is an issue in the rulebook labelled `proposal`, not a roadmap item and not a pull request; reuse alone does not qualify, a mixed idea splits. Write it generally and afresh, no product text or evidence. Until the issue exists the idea stays in the roadmap, `next` `pending transfer`. On opening, put the issue number in the handover: it owns the idea. At the next roadmap edit one final `retired` line, `lifted to the rulebook, #N`, replaces the item; reconsider on the issue, never here.

**A proposal's life.** Its body keeps `Priority: <level> — <reason>` (the levels in `library/changing-the-rulebook.md`), its decision, its next action and, once work starts, its pull request. The queue ranks by that priority, then dependencies (one waiting on another follows it), then oldest; no priority sorts last. It is debated in the head-office debate (`library/consultant.md`), a batch once 3 await debate or one has waited 7 days (trial thresholds), counted at an active session's opening, never on a timer; earlier when the current slice needs it; each settled and recorded on its own issue. No consultant: it stays pending and other work goes on. Agreement grants eligibility, not a pull request: that opens when work starts. An agreed proposal never outranks authorised revenue work. Until the Roadmap's live read has run (`library/roadmap.md`), the queue is the open issues labelled `proposal`; then its proposals card shows it (`library/board-proposals-card.md`), ranked by priority and age; it does not draw dependencies, so a session orders those from the issues themselves.

**Nothing is built before its review agrees**, roadmap idea or proposal, except a proven defect's repair; decisions reserved to him and the independent pull-request review stay as they are.

**At every close.** A product session's closing handover asks *Any material system improvement found?* "None" is valid; never invent one. One found is written as below, or as a proposal when shared.

**Writing one.** One line under `Ideas:` in the pull request's handover when it surfaces, copied into `roadmap.json` at the closing or parking roadmap edit: `id` `I<PR number><letter>` (letter per idea, never reused), `title` what, `plain` `Captured YYYY-MM-DD; why; surfaced by P7, #101`, `status` `proposed`, `level` P4 where it has levels, every other field null. It never blocks or widens the slice. Every distinct idea is written. First search by meaning the roadmap, `retired`, and rulebook proposals and pull requests, closed too: a match with new evidence updates its owner, the roadmap line keeping its first capture date, the issue by a comment; without it, nothing is written; an existing issue is linked, never duplicated. A violation of existing requirements, even one found in code, is a defect, repaired first, never an idea; investigate a suspected fault before classing it one.

**Review.** Asked for, never scheduled. At the opening roadmap read the CTO asks the consultant when no agreed item is ready, five or more `proposed` items stand, one has stood 30 days, or the Chairman asks; only ideas still due count; recorded outcomes (put to him, repair or agreed transfer pending) are due again only on his answer, new evidence or request. Agreed transfers resume when their obstacle clears. The consultant reads the live roadmap and `retired` first, asking if each is shared. One markdown exchange per product, every open idea in it, until the two agree; neither disposes of an idea alone. Its outcome goes in the handover and roadmap edit, not chat alone. Legacy `proposed` items count, dated as the item records, else "age unknown; reviewed YYYY-MM-DD". No consultant: the review stays pending, shipping goes on.

**Each idea ends as one of three.**

1. *Put to him*, when *Who decides* in `HOW-WE-BUILD.md` reserves it to him. Its `gate` begins `Decision needed:`; his word is *agreed* or *not yet*. *Agreed* makes it a normal P or A item, `plain` keeping the I-number, level set. *Not yet* is retired, with its reopening condition.
2. *A routine repair*: it restores what exists to what it claims and adds no capability. Its level is set at that review; it does not wait for product approval; checks, review and visual acceptance still apply. It stays `proposed`, outcome and repair pending in `next`; take it in the next agreed slice touching that area, or as its own defect slice if the failing case warrants; it goes when it merges.
3. *Retired*: one line in the roadmap's top-level `retired` list, started at the next roadmap edit where absent: `I101a, title, YYYY-MM-DD: reason; reopens when <condition or new evidence>`. When its condition or new evidence arrives, the idea returns to `proposed` for joint review.

**Kept small** (CHARTER 15). After a month, from the first review a product holds on or after 2 November 2026: its first idea written leaves its existing checks and the Roadmap green; every idea raised is in the roadmap, retired or merged; every review a trigger fired is done or marked pending; no technical question reached him. Review effort is then weighed against delivery, the proposals' debates and any delay to revenue work counted even when every one was handled correctly (decision 0020), and the page kept or reverted (CHARTER 13).

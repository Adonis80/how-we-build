# Voice Native

Scope: every Juku product's assistant, Juku OS included, either stack. Open when: work touches a product's assistant or its actions, or a screen's spoken path is mapped or built.

His ruling, 12 October 2026: *"Voice Native means every application is operable and configurable through natural two-way conversation with AI; the dialogue is kept as a chronological chat history; voice and visual act on one application state. Critical rule: Voice Native enhances and never replaces or compromises the visual interface; every product stays fully usable by touch, keyboard and mouse."* Agreed with the consultant in four rounds and approved by him ([#193](https://github.com/Adonis80/how-we-build/pull/193#issuecomment-6123262550)). Juku OS has nothing to declare under it until it has an assistant. **Built voice-ready, switched on when he chooses** (his ruling, 13 October 2026): from its first screen every product is built so that turning its assistant on is a switch, not an upgrade. Each screen action is named and reachable by code through the handler the screen uses, with the facts a read-back needs, and the product lists them; the assistant, any paid speech or model service, and anything advertised about voice wait for his switch. Every product can be operated and configured through natural two-way conversation with its assistant, typed or spoken, in the speaker's language. Conversation enhances the screen and never replaces or weakens it: everything stays fully usable by touch, keyboard and mouse, and a workflow declared voice-capable never secretly needs the screen.

1. **One state, one implementation.** The assistant reaches only actions the screens already perform, through the same routes, validation and permission checks, under the product's server-verified authority; a selected person is kept distinct from authenticated identity. The model proposes; ordinary code decides, computes and saves. Conversation adds no second implementation of a business rule. Adding voice does not require rebuilding existing screens. Each product lists its conversational actions beside the existing handlers they call.
2. **A yes approves one proposal.** Reads need no confirmation. Reversible draft edits may proceed with an accurate outcome and correction path; product rules may require stricter confirmation. Consequential actions require read-back of the material facts and a clear yes, spoken or tapped on the same card. Code holds the proposal: the action, validated inputs, record version, person and account. A correction, cancellation or change to those facts voids it. Ordinary code checks authority and the relevant state version as part of the write, refusing a changed proposal, and durably deduplicates its execution: a repeated yes or a retry returns the first result, and an unknown outcome is checked before any new attempt. A yes never grants what the person may not do. Saved and sent are separate outcomes.
3. **One conversation, in time order.** One thread per person per product, kept with the product's data under its existing retention, access and clearing rules (`RICH-DATA.md`). Speech becomes text; the text is the record. The screen shows the same thread, and typing is the same path as speaking. Summaries are disposable context, never proof. Help and builder requests keep existing masking; any raw contact data sent to a model is limited to the action that needs it, under permitted provider processing, and never enters help retrieval or later unrelated context.
4. **Current state wins.** The assistant reads current state when it needs it, including after its own actions. A screen change to a pending proposal voids it, and the assistant says so.
5. **A truthful next step, never an invented figure.** When unsure: one question, the nearest relevant thing with where it came from, an honest "not available yet", or, on the person's yes, a note to the builders. Figures come from validated user inputs, authorised records or deterministic calculations, never model invention.
6. **Bounded.** Code caps model calls, actions, input/output and time per turn, enforces the approved spending allowance before paid calls, and stops with an accurate, resumable outcome. Every attempt's usage and cost are recorded; unknown cost is reported as unknown.
7. **Proven per workflow.** Free CI tests with typed transcripts and a stubbed model prove the verified read result or saved outcome, covering missing data, correction, refusal, interruption and resumption; writes also cover stale and repeated confirmation and lost response where applicable. A real-model spoken preview journey in working noise proves the declared workflow without screen navigation once authenticated and listening is enabled. Each product declares coverage and the languages and devices demonstrated, never more. Required security and physical steps remain.

On a screen, the spoken path is mapped and drawn as `library/hypersolid.md` gives (step 2, and the sheet's row 2); this page is the law it is built and proved by.

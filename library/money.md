# Money out waits for money in

Scope: every product and session. Open when: anything would cost money or spend a capped allowance, or a product's hosting plan comes up.

**No paid plan, tool or service while a product earns nothing** (R-2026-09-21aa). It is not only the host's plan: **no session proposes a paid plan, a paid tool or a paid service while a product earns nothing**, and a feature that needs one is built on the free route or parked with the cost named. Where a paid lock would have enforced a rule, the rule still stands and is enforced by the checks and the review. **Before putting a cost to him, read what he has already decided** in that product's `PRODUCT.md` and `roadmap.json` alone (where they disagree, `PRODUCT.md` wins, the copy corrected in the same pull request); an answer he gives inside a product is not on the record until it reaches the repository ([Adonis80/Hemz-OS#68](https://github.com/Adonis80/Hemz-OS/pull/68)).

**The host's plan** (R-2026-09-17ab). No product is a real business until its revenue passes £1,000 a month. Until then it stays on the host's free plan and deploys there as normal: production is promoted, never held back waiting for a paid plan. The free plan's terms reserve it for non-commercial use; he was told, and the call is his. The paid plan is his to buy when a product's milestone fires, and no session buys it.

**The reviewer's cash is checked before it is spent (decision 0014; the amount is his, held privately).** Cash here is what the review key spends per request. Each request is checked first against the week's limit in the Actions secret `REVIEW_CASH_WEEKLY`, never a file, and against the key's own limit at the provider. A read it cannot check parks rather than guessing. So may one beside other reads in flight, each reserved at an estimate of its most (the output capped by the provider; the price the registry's, unenforced; the input at the 600 KB no request may pass, never the model's whole context), or beside its own finished requests, counted in full until the job ends. Plan allowance is recorded beside cash, never added to it. A read a limit refuses parks rather than falling back, saying which limit refused it (`REVIEW_CASH_WEEKLY` or the key's own), never the amount. A park that sent nothing says "nothing spent" and is asked again without `again`.

**An event-driven spender states its sums** (R-2026-10-13aa). When a change adds or alters anything that spends a paid or capped resource on events (a deploy, a read, an Actions run), or repairs a fault in one: (1) the calculation sits beside the changed code, as a comment the reviewer sees: events × fan-out × retries, the volume assumed, and the maximum enforced; unknown stays unknown; (2) a ceiling covers the shared resource, all its consumers and its period, the provider's own limit preferred to a local count; (3) a count it cannot read visibly skips discretionary spending, never blocks a rollback, and never claims a success it did not have. What was seen goes in the handover's cost line (`library/carries-sessions.md`). No list of meters is kept and no timer watches one.

# Money

Scope: every product. Open when: anything would cost money, or a product's revenue is counted.

**Money out waits for money in** (his ruling). No session proposes a paid plan, tool or service while a product earns nothing. A feature that needs one is built on the free route, or parked with the cost named. Where a paid lock would have enforced a rule, the rule still stands, enforced by the checks and the review.

**Read what he has already decided before putting a cost to him.** His product decisions go into `PRODUCT.md` and `roadmap.json`, so read those two. Where the roadmap's gate line disagrees with `PRODUCT.md`, `PRODUCT.md` wins, and the copy is corrected in the same pull request. An answer he gives inside a product is not on the record until it reaches the repository.

**The host's plan** (his ruling). A product is a real business once its revenue passes £1,000 a month. Until then it stays on the host's free plan and deploys there as normal; production is never held back for a paid plan. The free plan's terms reserve it for non-commercial use: he was told, and the call is his. The paid plan is his to buy when the milestone fires; no session buys it.

**Money milestones** live in `roadmap.json` (his ruling): each revenue figure he gives, and what he is reminded of when it is reached. At least: *revenue passes £1,000 a month: a real business, so move the host to its paid plan*. They are never build work. The slice that first counts a product's revenue shows it where he can see it, and checks the milestones every time it counts, so a reminder fires on its own.

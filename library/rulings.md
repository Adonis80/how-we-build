# Rulings

Scope: this rulebook and every product's rule pages. Open when: a rule's reason or his exact words are in question, or a ruling of his is added.

His words, once. Each entry is one ruling of the Chairman's on how we build: where it came from, the one place its rule now lives (`Home:`), and his words whole. A rule page states the rule in plain lines and cites the id; it never quotes him. A product keeps its own rulings in its own `RULINGS.md`. `product-reads/coherence.py` holds this: every dated ruling on a rule page cites an entry here, every entry's Home cites it back, and none of these words appear on a rule page.

## R-2026-10-02c · the spatial layer: light interactive 3D, its gestures, the Phena River
Source: juku-library/Juking_Style_3D_UI.md (#Added Juku Style spatial layer, #Phena River)
Home: library/hypersolid.md#Spatial layer
> The goal is to make our products feel like lightweight interactive 3D environments, closer to a lightweight game or cinematic spatial interface than a conventional website.
> Use Three.js/WebGL/WebGPU where appropriate, but avoid unnecessarily heavy 3D rendering.
> one-finger swipe/drag to pan, rotate or move through space; pinch to zoom in and out; tap objects to select them or move toward them; momentum/inertia where useful; optional subtle device-motion/parallax
> Apply this layer strongly to the Phena River. ... zoom out to see the broad landscape and major patterns; move spatially between clusters and themes; zoom progressively into individual experiences; continue into individual people, claims, transcripts, audio and supporting evidence; understand information hierarchy partly through physical depth, scale and position.
> Create the maximum perception of depth and spatial sophistication with the minimum rendering cost.

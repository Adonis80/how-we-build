# What every product carries: milestones, evidence, the product's pages

Scope: every product repository. Open when: a slice touches money milestones, evidence, the product's own pages, its README route or its design folder, or a handover reports a read's cost or the efficiency duty is weighed.

- Money milestones in `roadmap.json` (his ruling, 17 September 2026): each revenue figure he gives, and what he is reminded of when it is reached — at least *revenue passes £1,000 a month: a real business, so move the host to its paid plan*. Never build work. The slice that first counts a product's revenue shows it where he can see it and checks the milestones every time it counts, so a reminder fires on its own.
- Evidence recorded with its origin (his ruling, 17 September 2026): for anything the product learns from, whether it is a person's statement, a recorded observation or a derived result, along with where it came from, who recorded it and when, and under which notice. Kept only for a permitted purpose and period, with personal records and anything identifiable derived from them provably deletable, and any justified exception written down. The proof is the product's own test in the slice that changes the behaviour; a line in this list is not a machine gate.
- The handover's `evidence` field, which `HOW-WE-BUILD.md` names with the rest: what evidence this slice captures, or deliberately does not capture; which decision it can improve; and what cost or retention obligation it adds. "None, because ..." is a valid answer, which is what stops it becoming a box everyone ticks.
- After a read, the handover carries one sourced line: reported cost (not cash), final attempts only, by check-run link and commit, marked complete, partial or unknown, never zero for missing.
- The efficiency duty (`library/carries-sessions.md`) is the CTO's, judged at the month review `library/ideas-for-later.md` names (the first on or after 2 November 2026) as one experiment with that page (CHARTER 13): kept only if the saving actually seen (tokens or cash measured, never nominal prices or work avoided) exceeds the recorded cost of measuring, extra reviews and rework, with delivery delay and escaped defects checked; otherwise it alone is reverted (CHARTER 13).
- `PRODUCT.md` and `NAMES.md`: what the product is, and the words it uses for its own things.
- A `README.md` route section saying that a session attached to the repo at its start works in it directly, a session started without it goes through the Mac, and the ten-second test tells which.
- A Project whose instructions are the template (`library/project-template-product.md`), whole, and nothing else.
- A `design/` folder, for a product with a user interface: its own constitution on one capped, machine-checked page holding only what is true there. Agreed screen designs live in the product's library, `<product>-library/design/<screen>/`, never in `design/` (`library/screen-design.md`). A draft pull request's handover records the brief sent, the round and the last two pictures; the reviewer judges a screen against its builder-only note and the screen law, and the look is his alone to judge. The screen law and the rubric are read from this repository, never copied down; whoever draws a screen, or a prototype or picture of one for the Chairman, reads the screen law and the constitution first. Its check holds the shape: one constitution page, and the product's check allows each screen's folder in its library. A product that has not yet adopted the page works to `design/ARCHITECT.md` and the spec template until its next pull request makes it current; the first screen through the five steps is what proves them. This repository's own board is out of scope: `juku-library/` is flat and public.

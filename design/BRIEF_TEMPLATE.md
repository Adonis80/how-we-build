# Brief — <task name>, <date>

*Written by the CTO from current product truth. Source commit: `<sha>`. One coherent task or journey per brief. One brief exists at a time; it is deleted when its spec lands — a brief is an input, never a record.*

- **Who:** the roles using it, and which comes first.
- **The task:** the real-world thing being done, where it starts, what must be true at the end.
- **Fixed rules:** the business truth that cannot move (the product's non-negotiables always restated here).
- **Data:** required; optional; already known (never re-asked).
- **Permissions:** who may see, do, lock what.
- **States that matter:** the conditions that change understanding or action — empty, unknown, partial, confirmed, failed, restricted.
- **Depends on:** screens, systems, figures this touches.
- **Constraints:** file, framework, tokens, devices.
- **Success, measurably:** e.g. a first-timer reaches the outcome in N seconds on the common path.
- **Non-goals:** what this task must leave alone.

Describe the problem, not the layout. A layout appears here only if the Chairman already approved it.
